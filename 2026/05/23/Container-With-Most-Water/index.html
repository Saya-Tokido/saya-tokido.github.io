11. Container With Most Water - LeetCode

2. Problem Description

You are given an integer array height of length n. There are n vertical lines drawn such that the two endpoints of the i-th line are (i, 0) and (i, height[i]).

Find two lines that together with the x-axis form a container, such that the container contains the most water.

Return the maximum amount of water a container can store.

Notice that you may not slant the container.

Example 1:

  • Input: height = [1,8,6,2,5,4,8,3,7]
  • Output: 49
  • Explanation: The vertical lines are represented by array [1,8,6,2,5,4,8,3,7]. In this case, the max area of water (the blue section) the container can contain is 7 * (8 - 1) = 49.

Example 2:

  • Input: height = [1,1]
  • Output: 1

Constraints:

  • n == height.length
  • 2 <= n <= 10^5
  • 0 <= height[i] <= 10^4

3. Core Idea

  • Approach: Two-Pointers (Greedy strategy).
  • Mechanism:
    • We initialize two pointers: l at the beginning (0) and r at the end (height.size() - 1) of the array.
    • The area of the container is always restricted by the shorter line: Area = (r - l) * min(height[l], height[r]).
    • To maximize the area, we try to find a taller line by moving the pointer pointing to the shorter side. Moving the pointer pointing to the taller side would never increase the area, because the width decreases by 1 and the height is bounded by the shorter line anyway.
